## Open Threads

### Magic-System Revision: Compare Coherent Packages of Changes

**Revision goals**
- Retain compositional, engineering-based magic while making mana a more capable natural computing substrate and mental control more direct
- Make arguably conscious magical agents practically achievable through deliberate development, with accessible cantrips and specialist spell engineering remaining distinct
- Preserve scarcity, meaningful resistance to hostile interference, and costly remote access; exclude one-mage armies and intrinsic symbolic causation
- Keep broad implementation changes available; [[99 Workshop/Magic-System Revision|Magic-System Revision]] owns the detailed author-confirmed criteria and unselected revision packages

**Next steps and why**
- Compare the ranked packages against the recorded criteria, beginning with native mana computation and reciprocal brain–soul cognition because these address the central control and golemancy obstacles
- Develop throughput, access protection, and reliable cantrip construction alongside any increase in autonomy so the intended limits survive the enabling changes
- Use concrete casting, golem, mind-magic, and remote-device cases to choose capabilities and mechanisms without requiring a complete microscopic model
- Keep engineered magical distance as a separate open branch; consider deeper unification of mana states only if it makes selected behaviours clearer
- Revisit affected foundational references and their dependencies only once a direction is selected and reference edits are authorized

**Where things stand:** the author answered the brainstorming questionnaire and confirmed the broad preference summary. Detailed criteria and ranked candidate packages are recorded in the working note. The recommendations are unselected; no new mechanics have been adopted. Workshop and context maintenance are authorized, while setting-reference rewrites await a selected direction and authorization.

**Clarified boundaries:** intent does not supply missing spell logic, and learning requires designed support. The author leans against imparting an unexplained mundane skill as a shortcut to novel spell engineering. Sentiment can affect mental state but does not create magical connections. Earlier broader suggestions about skill imprinting or sympathetic relationships should not be treated as accepted directions.

**Loose ends:** the substrate's native operations, its mental interface, brain–soul division of cognition, requirements for artificial cognitive development, remnant persistence, sustainable processing and actuation costs, mind-magic access, and the relation between prepared endpoints, density, field geometry, and remote operations
