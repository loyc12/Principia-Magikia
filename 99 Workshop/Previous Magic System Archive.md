# Previous Magic System Archive

**Type: Archive navigation**

**Status: Active**

**Scope: The complete magic-system reference collection before the current refoundation**

**Development: Archived and superseded for active development**

The 17 notes formerly under `10 Magic System` are preserved in `99 Workshop/Previous Magic System`, with their original hierarchy, content, approval labels, open questions, and `VALIDATE` prompts. Links to those notes now resolve to their archived owners. Internal links use explicit archive paths so future references can reuse filenames without redirecting the old account.

The original status labels record the notes' former approval state. In particular, **Current foundation** inside this archive does not make an old rule a foundation of the replacement. Archival neither rejects every old mechanism nor approves its migration. Consult old mechanics only when their subjects arise during writing, and remove archived files only after their content has been discarded or integrated at the author's direction.
