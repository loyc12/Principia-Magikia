## Integration Questions

These identify interfaces to resolve, not selected solutions or a requirement to settle every topic before proceeding.

- **Mago-physics and souls:** reconcile soul formation, substrate-dependent cognition, and disruptive galvanic coupling with the chosen account of mana and its interactions
- **Concepts and execution:** establish how personal representations become usable spell inputs or procedures without supplying missing knowledge or unrestricted truth detection
- **Universal access and physical limits:** reconcile noospheric universality and optional lenses with signalling, access protection, costs, and the no-time-travel axiom
- **Autonomy, survival, and power:** distinguish cognitive support from retained imprints, and evaluate how tools and resources enable exceptional practitioners without removing practical limits
- **Personal mastery and shared methods:** make individual understanding and tool familiarity compatible with reproducible instruction, interoperability, and standardization

As discussions resolve these questions, record the chosen interaction, its source, affected topics, unresolved conflicts, and reference changes needed. Leave unsupported details open rather than silently importing an older package.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Develop the semi-independent brainstorms, beginning next with mago-physical implementation choices, and compare their interfaces here
2. After brainstorming, copy broadly reusable terms such as construct and spell into [[00 Core/04 Terminology|Terminology]], with simplified meanings and provenance, preserving the originals
3. Archive the existing system intact before substantive reconstruction, preserving internal references and approval state, and establish the archive boundary and active navigation
4. Translate selected, compatible principles into replacement references, tracing affected dependencies and retaining explicit deferrals

Terminology copying and archival remain pending. The archive boundary and destinations have not been selected. Current setting references remain in place; their mechanisms do not dictate the replacement. Optional magical cognitive substrates remain deferred unless the author chooses to develop them.
