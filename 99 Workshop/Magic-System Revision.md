## Integration Questions

These identify interfaces to resolve, not selected solutions or a requirement to settle every topic before proceeding.

- **Mago-physics and souls:** reconcile soul formation, substrate-dependent cognition, and disruptive galvanic coupling with the chosen account of mana and its interactions
- **Concepts and execution:** establish how conceptual chains in soul-forms direct mana and how duplicated patterns become usable spell-forms without supplying missing knowledge or unrestricted truth detection
- **Spell-form support and access:** establish independent maintenance, pattern transfer, remote interaction, access protection, and costs while preserving the no-time-travel axiom; the remaining universal-noosphere package is discarded from active development
- **Autonomy, survival, and power:** distinguish true cognition, autonomous self-modifying webs, and retained imprints, and evaluate how tools and resources enable exceptional practitioners without removing practical limits
- **Personal mastery and shared methods:** make individual understanding and tool familiarity compatible with reproducible instruction, interoperability, and standardization

As discussions resolve these questions, record the chosen interaction, its source, affected topics, unresolved conflicts, and reference changes needed. Leave unsupported details open rather than silently importing an older package.

## Implementation Sequence

1. Archive the complete old magic system before reconstruction, preserving content, links, approval state, and review prompts; establish the archive boundary and active navigation
2. Keep affected non-magic-system files outside the archive, add a header warning to author-facing notes, and redirect their old-system links to archived owners; report corpus references without editing corpus text
3. Write replacement references from the selected foundation, resolving mechanisms only as needed, consulting old mechanics case by case, and extracting terminology gradually with author approval for additional terms
4. Later remove archived files only after their content is fully discarded or integrated
