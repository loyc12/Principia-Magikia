# Magic-System Revision

**Type: Development working note**

**Status: Exploring**

**Scope: Author-confirmed design preferences and unselected revision options**

**Approval: Required before adopting new mechanics into setting references**

The author has reopened major magic-system development before further story development. This note records the author's questionnaire answers, their subsequent confirmation of the broad preference summary, and candidate changes proposed in response. Approval of the goals is not approval of any implementation below. The numbered references remain the current foundation pending selected revisions. [[99 Workshop/00 Current Work Context|Current Work Context]] owns continuation order and parked work.

## Author-Confirmed Revision Criteria

### Identity and Scope

- Retain mana particles, their declensions, their organization into constructs, and constructs' capacity to hold and execute spell logic as the strongest starting commitments
- Other mechanisms and restrictions may be retained, revised, replaced, or removed when the justification or replacement is compelling
- Preserve rigorous, compositional magic while making its natural substrate substantially more capable and its results more recognizably magical
- Combine physics, chemistry, biology, computing, robotics, and structural engineering, with practitioners specializing across these areas
- Existing practitioners should possess substantial knowledge, while a methodological renaissance and magical industrial revolution remain consequential possibilities

### Capabilities and Practitioner Progression

- Arguably conscious magical agents must be practically achievable within the story, rather than requiring an independently solved artificial brain before magic contributes meaningfully
- Spell engineering should resemble programming and cultivating a naturally occurring computing substrate more than constructing every mechanical and computational component from scratch
- Minds should interact with magic more directly than through low-level soul-form manipulation of local mana
- Ordinary people use roughly five to ten common cantrips for such tasks as heating, lighting, and cleaning without eliminating scarcity or trivializing survival
- Novices use more specialized cantrips and begin adjusting them; advanced practitioners design spells; experts develop healing, golemancy, communication infrastructure, engineering, and warfare applications

### Intent, Construction, and Execution

- Effects remain compositions of smaller attribute-powered or intrinsic mana operations
- A command such as catching an object requires an existing alias, decoding arrangement, or agent that resolves it into selection, bounded operations, feedback, and stopping conditions
- Intent alone does not supply missing spell logic, knowledge, or competence
- The author leans against directly imparting an unexplained mundane skill as a substitute for engineering a novel spell
- Designers must understand the components they use and their interactions, without necessarily reconstructing every established component from first principles
- Executing an existing formula requires relevant fundamentals and instructions, not the designer's complete understanding
- Learning fundamentals should resemble mastering rigorous structured material; expert-designed cantrips should subsequently be accessible and resilient
- Most alterations to intricate spells should cause failure rather than useful innovation; contextual bugs and unintended effects remain possible
- Public cantrips prioritize restricted parameters, resilience, and safe failure under accumulated copying and casting errors
- Informal transmission can accumulate casting dialects and is discouraged or regulated in the author's intended model

### Brain and Soul-Form Direction

The following is an author-proposed direction whose details remain open, rather than a selected mechanism.

- Native brains and soul-forms develop together; an Arrival experiences disruptive rapid soul-form formation and subsequent adaptation
- Bidirectional coupling initially interferes with brain function but can become compensated for or beneficial through mutual adaptation
- Possible benefits include improved neural transmission, reduced physiological cost, and more direct brain-to-mana control; none is selected in detail
- A remnant preferentially preserves pathways, habitual organization, and deeply reinforced patterns rather than a complete autobiographical record
- Transient state decays faster than persistent organization; repeatedly used information might become embedded in that organization
- Some retained information may survive if it has not been overwritten or degraded, so the intended distinction is not an absolute prohibition on all memory
- Appropriate input or mental context might elicit source-like behaviour without restoring the source's complete mind
- Unsupported soul-forms ultimately decay, with mana-rich environments potentially accelerating disruption
- The author's computing analogy expresses differential persistence, not a literal commitment to a particular stack, heap, or memory architecture

### Autonomy and Personhood

- Autonomy depends on deliberately engineered architecture, ranging from fixed routines to adaptive networks
- A cleaning implement usually needs simple bounded control, not reasoning or personhood; some cantrips may simply assist the user's physical work
- Learning or self-modification requires designed support rather than arising inevitably through age
- Unmanaged drift usually produces neutral or harmful quirks; maintenance and realignment remain useful local trades
- Deliberate customization can produce distinctive behaviour without establishing consciousness
- Tools and possible persons occupy a continuum, but full magical personhood is unheard of and its possibility uncertain at the story's opening
- The protagonist should be among the first, possibly the first, to create an arguably independent person through sustained improvement of an all-purpose golem and knowledge brought from Earth

### Mind Magic

- Favour emotional influence and detection, vague sensations, mental imagery, and similarly imprecise exchanges
- Repeated practice between familiar participants can improve precision substantially
- Individual mental organization complicates interpretation even within a species; cross-species work, especially with non-sapient animals, can be exceptionally difficult
- Twin practitioners are an author-suggested case of potentially strong mutual communication, not a selected automatic link
- Animic resistance remains substantial; cooperative practitioners can make access easier by managing their own boundaries, while hostile access remains difficult
- Familiarity helps interpretation but should not automatically bypass resistance
- Sentiment can affect mental state and repeatability, but love, names, ownership, and symbolic significance do not independently create magical access

### Distance and Field Behaviour

- Magical distance remains a major barrier, even if it differs from material distance
- Remote operation must require substantial preparation, infrastructure, elevated running costs, or an appropriate combination
- Prefer one underlying explanation for non-portal remote interactions if a coherent model can support it
- Artificial, device-relative changes to magical distance are more important than naturally shifting magical geography, although both are attractive
- Endpoint acquisition, prepared pairing, cord requirements, and the relation between information transfer and direct operation remain undecided
- Possible mechanisms include jointly prepared mana, selective structural channels, cords that modify field distance, and density-dependent field geometry
- Dimensional space bridging may remain distinct or share a deeper basis with field behaviour
- Primed mana as a weak undifferentiated expression of several attributes is an exploratory possibility, not a commitment

### Power, Failure, and Dissemination

- Exclude mages capable of independently replacing an entire military through overwhelming power and versatility
- Exclude trivial unimpeded attacks on bodies, minds, or spells, including passive wards and distant attacks
- Preserve contextual failure modes and the consequences of design quality, operating conditions, and maintenance
- Spells become readily shareable through notation understandable to their intended recipients, sometimes requiring adaptation into that notation
- Representational limits, specialist conventions, and audience competence affect what can be communicated reliably
- Simple public formulas and specialist design descriptions serve different users; knowing a cantrip does not confer its designer's expertise

## Ranked Candidate Packages

These ranks express recommendations, not author selections or a mandatory implementation sequence. Package 3 accompanies any major increase in autonomy or remote capability even though the central enabling changes rank above it. Package 4 should be developed alongside the first two. Package 5 can remain undecided while local cognition and casting are resolved. Package 6 is optional.

### 1. Native Mana Computation and Direct Mental Interfaces

**Recommendation: Highest priority**

Current constructs already contain sensing, processing, state, feedback, and reusable components. The missing step is a sufficiently capable physical account of these facilities that does not require separately built electronic or chemical equivalents for sophisticated cognition.

- Expand coherent mana structures into a configurable substrate with stable signals, retained state, recurrence, and optional plasticity
- Make common computational and regulatory motifs cultivable or reusable without determining every particle position
- Rework mental control into a calibrated interface able to configure components, connect permitted interfaces, inspect state, and supply parameters
- Preserve a real instantiation process: knowing a formula alone does not create a running construct
- Allow materials to support anchorage, sensing, protection, mana supply, and persistent records without requiring every operation to be realized as a separate material machine
- Retain compositional effects and explicitly engineered interpretation; a desired outcome is not executable merely because someone imagines it

This can build on existing intrinsic mana interactions rather than immediately adding a mental attribute or allowing every attribute to operate on mana. Fixed constructs need not become adaptive. A programmable substrate makes cognitive architecture feasible but does not supply an already trained mind.

**Dependencies and affected owners:** [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/01 Core Principles|Core Principles]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/02 Mana Dynamics|Mana Dynamics]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/05 Mana Constructs|Mana Constructs]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/07 Spell Composition|Spell Composition]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/08 Mana Casting|Mana Casting]], and the soul-form changes below

### 2. Reciprocal Cognition and Artificial Animic Development

**Recommendation: Highest priority alongside Package 1**

- Replace the absolute requirement for physical neurological impulses with requirements for appropriate recurrent organization, support, and coupling that engineered mana networks can also meet
- Replace the brain's exclusive ownership of cognition with a coupled brain and soul-form model, while leaving the allocation of memory and computation open
- Distinguish a supporting substrate from a functioning brain: a vessel may sustain a cognitive mana structure without independently duplicating its cognition
- Let formation, training, and repeated use stabilize some pathways more strongly than transient state
- Explain how available mana sustains a structure while uncontrolled environmental mana can disrupt it, avoiding the assumption that higher concentration always preserves remnants better
- Develop precise mind magic as difficult coupling and interpretation between different organizations, with resistance separate from interpretive familiarity
- Replace categorical golem classifications that exclude mana-based cognition with an architectural continuum, while retaining the distinction between autonomy and evidence of consciousness

An incremental golem could begin with declared perception, movement, and task routines, then acquire deliberately designed learning, persistent memory, and broader integration. Modern computing knowledge helps the protagonist work on those problems; it does not substitute for experimental development, training, compatible materials, or local expertise.

**Dependencies and affected owners:** Package 1, [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/03 Soul-Forms|Soul-Forms]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/04 Somaturgy|Somaturgy]], [[10 Magic System/02 Additional Disciplines/Golemancy|Golemancy]], animic resistance in [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/06 Spell Subjects|Spell Subjects]], and the corresponding overview and core principles

### 3. Throughput, Access, and Sustainable Autonomy

**Recommendation: Required companion to increased capabilities**

- Separate information storage, active control, physical work, and channel capacity instead of pricing all complexity as continuous expensive casting
- Permit cheap durable organization under suitable conditions while assigning costs to processing rate, precision, repair, actuation, and environmental exposure
- Limit practical scaling through mana collection and delivery, dissipation, material support, interference, maintenance, and accessible operating volume
- Keep locating a target, establishing a channel, interpreting it, and altering it as distinct requirements
- Extend meaningful protection to construct state and control interfaces so knowing a design or channel does not authorize rewriting it
- Retain ordinary vulnerabilities through external physical consequences; animic defence need not prevent falling debris or fire outside the body
- Evaluate autonomous weapons, stockpiled reservoirs, ambient mana collection, and mundane energy sources as well as directly supervised combat spells

Concentration cannot be the main military limit once machines can perform the supervision. The relevant boundary is between an individual caster and an industrial system of supply, construction, repair, and deployment. Infrastructure may be powerful without one unaided mage replacing an army. Precise scales remain to be chosen, rather than asserted through an unexplained universal cap.

**Dependencies and affected owners:** Packages 1 and 2, [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/02 Mana Dynamics|Mana Dynamics]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/05 Mana Constructs|Mana Constructs]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/06 Spell Subjects|Spell Subjects]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/09 Casting Practices|Casting Practices]], and later the chosen remote mechanism

### 4. Spell Languages, Reusable Designs, and Reliable Instantiation

**Recommendation: Strong; develop with Packages 1 and 2**

- Expand the existing distinction between design formulas, templates, rehearsed reconstruction, and running spell-forms
- Let notation systems express some operations naturally and others awkwardly; translation can require engineering rather than symbol substitution
- Make public cantrips restricted interfaces over robust designs, with checks before significant mana admission and bounded failure during execution
- Keep structural or type-like validation distinct from proof of intended behaviour; a well-formed spell can still contain a semantic error
- Allow casting aids to guide construction without making ordinary written symbols intrinsically executable
- Make reusable libraries, diagnostic instruments, calibration practices, and better teaching important advances available through collaboration
- Distinguish copying a working artefact from transmitting an inspectable, adaptable design; notation remains valuable without universally banning construct duplication

This largely expands existing strengths rather than overturning them. It preserves the author's recipe and spreadsheet comparisons and avoids the earlier suggestion that a mundane skill could simply be impressed into a new spell without magical engineering.

**Dependencies and affected owners:** [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/07 Spell Composition|Spell Composition]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/08 Mana Casting|Mana Casting]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/11 Magical Training|Magical Training]], and Packages 1 and 3 for actual interfaces and validation limits

### 5. Engineered Magical Distance and Prepared Remote Access

**Recommendation: Promising separate branch; choose after clarifying local interfaces**

Current Resonance already transmits along the mana field at finite speed, and Dimensional magic already permits prepared path modification. The proposed addition is controllable, device-relative shortening of magical routes and possibly broader use of those routes.

- Give the mana field effective distances that need not match material distances
- Prefer organized mana structure as the means of controlling useful shortening, with density setting strength or capacity rather than determining a whole route by itself
- Treat prepared endpoint selection, route establishment, and sustained carrying capacity as separate engineering problems
- Explore information, mana supply, and remote operation as uses of the same channel, with different demands and endpoint arrangements
- Keep material passage a stronger or distinct Dimensional operation unless broader unification proves useful
- Preserve resistance at the destination; shortening a route does not make the endpoint body or construct cooperative
- Require accounting for setup work, maintaining anchors, route length, motion of devices, capacity, disruption, and recovery after failure

**Preferred variant:** structured mana can reshape the field's effective metric. Cords are one possible stabilizing architecture, not yet a required universal mechanism. Density can strengthen the effect, while arrangement determines useful direction and endpoints. Background concentrations may introduce smaller disturbances.

**Broader alternative:** density alone changes field geometry substantially. This is worth exploring, but would affect reservoirs, soul-forms, wards, cities, and the mana cycle. The model must avoid an unchecked loop in which concentration shortens distance, which increases concentration, which shortens distance again. Attunement or discharge may change the geometry, so ordinary consumption could disturb nearby links.

Metric shortening does not by itself create a new topological connection. If devices establish a new route rather than deform an existing one, that is an additional rule to select. Prepared resonance is an addressing relation, not automatically a shortcut. A common channel principle need not mean that information, mana particles, and matter all cross equally easily.

Finite travel along the magical route and the no-time-travel axiom remain constraints. The model need not inherit every space-time implication of real-world metric engineering.

**Dependencies and affected owners:** Package 3, [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/01 Core Principles|Core Principles]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/02 Mana Dynamics|Mana Dynamics]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/05 Mana Constructs|Mana Constructs]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/06 Spell Subjects|Spell Subjects]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/12 Attribute Theory|Attribute Theory]], and the optional quantitative appendix [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/10 Mana Physics|Mana Physics]]. A density-wide model would also affect the cosmological mana cycle

### 6. Deeper Unification of Mana States and Operations

**Recommendation: Optional; lower priority than the targeted changes**

- Consider Attuned states as specialized, efficient regimes of a broader mana interaction, with Primed mana supporting weak precursor behaviour under organized conditions
- Alternatively retain the current operational split and broaden intrinsic Primed interactions only enough to support computation, neural coupling, and field geometry
- Revisit the prohibition on attributes acting upon mana selectively if it obstructs useful interfaces; direct mana manipulation need not mean unrestricted control of another person's constructs
- Preserve a reason to obtain and maintain specialized attributes; unrestricted Primed operation risks making them redundant
- If precursor effects perform work, define where their potentia goes and how the structure is depleted or sustained
- Reconsider absolute restrictions on particle properties or scaffold geometry only when they obstruct a selected mechanism, rather than reopening every microscopic detail

The narrower alternative is currently recommended. Packages 1, 2, and 5 do not require Primed mana to be a weak version of every attribute. Full unification could be elegant, but it would require revisiting the mana cycle, Expenditure, affinities, costs, and material consequences together.

**Dependencies and affected owners:** [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/01 Core Principles|Core Principles]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/02 Mana Dynamics|Mana Dynamics]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/12 Attribute Theory|Attribute Theory]], [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/13 Material Affinities|Material Affinities]], and the quantitative appendix, plus whichever cognition or field mechanisms it is meant to explain

## Comparison and Next Decisions

The recommended initial combination is Packages 1 and 2, accompanied by Package 3 and developed through Package 4. It addresses cognition, control, accessibility, and the protagonist's golem without requiring a selected remote mechanism. Package 5 is the next independent design branch. Package 6 should be adopted only if it explains several selected behaviours more clearly than separate intrinsic interactions.

Use a public heating cantrip, a configurable spell component, an incrementally improved golem, a cooperative mental exchange, and a prepared remote device pair as comparison cases. Ask what structure is required, what the operator must understand, what resources the operation consumes, what prevents hostile access, and what failure looks like. These are design probes, not new canonical examples or a full-system audit.

Do not import the earlier unselected suggestion of effortless skill imprinting or sentiment-created sympathy. Do not infer that every adaptive construct has a soul-form or consciousness. Preserve existing explicit development deferrals unless a selected package requires reopening a specific item, such as scaffold form and function or somatic control.
