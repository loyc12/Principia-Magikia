## Earlier Author Preferences and Reversals

The earlier approach favoured mana particles, attribute-like declensions, constructs that execute spell logic, compositional magic, and stronger natural computational affordances. It sought recognizable magical results and substantial personal progression, with existing practitioners already skilled and an Arrival-induced methodological renaissance still consequential. The new particle foundation retains particles and structures but does not inherit the old attribute architecture or energy accounting.

Earlier preferences included roughly five to ten common cantrips for ordinary practitioners, novice formula modification, and specialist design work. Those numbers and stages remain revisable. Engineering should rely on learned components without demanding that every user rediscover them. Directly imparting an unexplained mundane skill was disfavoured. [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/11 Magical Learning and Mastery|Magical Learning and Mastery]] and [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/12 Spell Composition and Sharing|Spell Composition and Sharing]] owns the useful current direction.

The old prohibition on equipped one-mage armies is **superseded**. The current direction allows them in principle while making knowledge, resources, and tools consequential. The old proposal for a protagonist-built arguably independent golem is historical narrative material, not a requirement of the replacement. The cognitive-substrate constraint still governs claims of true consciousness; autonomous self-modifying conceptual webs are now allowed as possible pseudo-minds.

## Leads for Later Reassessment

These are unselected candidates, not tasks to complete before archival or initial writing.

- **Soul development:** native brains and soul-forms might develop together, while an Arrival's rapid formation causes temporary disruption and adaptation; possible benefits to signalling or control remain unselected
- **Remnants:** habits and reinforced patterns may persist longer than transient state, with fragments of memory or source-like responses possible without establishing full identity or continuity
- **Mind magic:** emotional influence, vague sensations, imagery, and exchange between familiar participants were favoured; cross-species interpretation and hostile access remain difficult, while familiarity does not itself bypass resistance
- **Autonomy:** bounded tools need no personhood; deliberate architecture may support feedback, learning, customization, or pseudo-mind behaviour, while ordinary ageing and unmanaged drift do not imply intelligence
- **Computational media:** candidate mana structures could support signals, retained state, recurrence, and optional plasticity, with materials used for anchoring, sensing, storage, supply, and protection
- **Spell practice:** design notation, rehearsed templates, and running structures may differ; translation, diagnostics, calibration, restricted inputs, and bounded failure are candidates, with structural validity distinct from useful behaviour
- **Remote access:** distance was intended as a major barrier; prepared endpoints, cords, selective channels, and engineered field geometry were considered, with setup, throughput, movement, disruption, and recovery unresolved
- **Magical distance:** one branch gave structured routes an effect on field distance; another made density alter geometry, raising feedback questions around reservoirs, wards, cities, and the mana cycle; addressing alone was not a shortcut or a new topological connection
- **Scale and protection:** assess power supplies, stockpiles, unattended devices, operating volume, interference, maintenance, and the distinct steps of locating, connecting to, interpreting, and altering a subject
- **Natural phenomena:** an unselected hypothesis proposed spontaneous effects from mana interactions before minds exist, with conceptual chains directing the same interactions for deliberate casting
- **Physical operations:** older ideas included weak undifferentiated operations, specialist efficiencies, and selective operations upon mana; the current foundation supersedes their old classification without deciding every replacement operation

## Discarded Noosphere Package

**Provenance:** the former Noosphere, Souls, and Minds brainstorm explored a universal shared conceptual web, personal and local lenses, reinforcement through minds, religious pseudo-agents, and a subtle hand of fate. It also speculated about shared beliefs, access to collective patterns, artificial contributions, and ways noospheric change might be sensed or signalled. These proposals were later separated from personal soul-held conceptual webs, then **discarded from active development by the author**. They are not layers of the current universal catalogue of fundamental conceptual nodes.

The author's later preference, if a universal noosphere were ever reconsidered, was that extracting meaningful information by detecting its changes be virtually impossible. This superseded earlier permissive suggestions about reading collective propositions or deliberate signalling. This is provenance, not a current alternative to develop. A possible conceptual entity inside the Singularity is a **separate deferred proposal** in [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/03 Mana Circulation#Separate Deferred Entity|Mana Circulation]]; it does not restore the collective-web mechanism.
