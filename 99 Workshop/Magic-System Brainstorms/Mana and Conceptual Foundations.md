## Selected Foundation

### Mana Field and Particle Properties

The mana field is an additional layer of the Expanse. Mana particles are excitation nodes within it. Particles are conserved. Energy conservation is not imposed for now; this does not supply a selected mechanism for creating energy or replenishing mana. Mana waves and their possible role in field transport or energy accounting are deferred.

Three particle properties organize the new model:

- **Action Potentia:** the amount of magical energy a particle can supply to an effect
- **Connection Potentia:** a scalar connection capacity with quantized values corresponding to available slots around a particle; compatible particles fit into those slots mutually, preferably with available slots on both participants
- **Conceptual Attunement:** optional compatibility with a fundamental conceptual node, improving the usefulness or efficiency of Action Potentia for compatible actions while narrowing its possible uses

Connection Potentia draws on the previous bonding idea, whose terms included coherence wells and monadic, dyadic, triadic, tetradic, and polyadic coordination. This does not import the old account intact: the previous coordination states depended on local conditions rather than fixed particle valences. The source of Connection Potentia and the details of mutual bonding remain deferred.

Unattuned mana is the default spell power supply. Specialized attunements chiefly support the application of actions. They are optional for small or simple actions but intended to become practically essential for complex or large actions, including operations requiring exotic attunements. Greater precision and denser or more readily available Action Potentia are proposed advantages, not selected quantitative mechanisms. Conversion, compatibility, mixed supplies, and the exact advantages remain deferred.

Direct magical effects upon mana are explicitly possible. The old prohibition on selecting mana particles or structures as operational subjects is discarded. The operation catalogue and its detailed limits remain undeveloped.

### Conceptual Nodes and Organization

The former phrase **conceptual space** now means the universal catalogue of possible fundamental conceptual nodes permitted by the system's laws. It is not an additional place or an inhabited repository. The author's comparisons are the IPA's inventory of phonemes and the periodic table's description of permitted atomic forms.

Fundamental nodes need not correspond to familiar whole concepts. They may be esoteric building blocks from which personal concepts are assembled. **Fly** is composite. As illustrative decompositions rather than selected nodes, **up** might combine direction, away, and base, or direction, near, and centre. The actual node inventory has not been designed.

Personal conceptual webs organize these building blocks through the host's experience and understanding. Conceptual chains combine concepts, criteria, and procedures to enact magic. Shared fundamental nodes do not supply missing procedures, observations, or personal context.

The intended practice resembles low-level programming: practitioners organize learned concepts and criteria into executable chains rather than engineering every effect through detailed particle physics. The mana field may provide the mechanism connecting conceptual organization, attunement, and effects. The earlier suggestion that nearby concept-associated sources naturally attune particles remains tentative. Birds producing flow-, movement-, or flight-associated mana were exploratory examples, not adopted attunements or a selected detection mechanism. Both natural and artificial attunement mechanisms are deferred, including whether conceptual proximity is used at all.

### Mana-Forms and Casting

**Mana-forms** is the working umbrella term for coherent mana assemblies, including soul-forms and spell-forms. Particles may flow freely or assemble through their connection capacity. Natural assembly occurs where sufficient mana concentration and connection-enabling conditions coincide. Living beings, particularly active neurological systems, provide such conditions for soul-form formation. Other sources remain possible. Coherent assembly alone does not establish a soul-form or consciousness.

Soul-forms retain the personal conceptual webs and mind coupling described in [[99 Workshop/Magic-System Brainstorms/Souls, Minds, and Conceptual Magic|the conceptual working note]]. The strengthened bond supports a trainable additional mana sense, potentially at finer levels, alongside refined mana and conceptual manipulation. Exact resolution and mechanisms remain open. The intended disruption of naive Earth-style galvanic computers remains, with thresholds and mitigation unresolved.

Unstructured casting executes conceptual chains through the practitioner's soul-form. Structured casting externalizes conceptual organization into spell-forms, supporting wards, tools, engines, autonomous constructs, and golems. Soul-forms and spell-forms must both support conceptual manipulation, guided by minds or by conceptual webs and chains acting upon other conceptual organization. How representation, triggering, observation, execution, and stopping work is deferred. This is a priority for development after the base system is established.

**Tentative assembly distinction:** static assemblies could resemble rigid objects, while dynamic assemblies could combine a firmer portion with a flexible, soup-like portion responsive to concepts and neuronal or galvanic activity. The distinction and its mechanisms remain deferred.

### Circulation and Protection

The broad mana cycle is retained. For now, **Spent mana goes up towards the Singularity; charged mana goes down** is the working rule of thumb. Primed and Spent remain preferred labels for maximal and minimal charge, but two potentia properties require later clarification of their coverage. The old permutation package is not migrated by retaining these labels.

The following distribution ideas are tentative and deferred:

- Low Action Potentia and low Connection Potentia correspond to Spent mana moving towards the Singularity
- High action and low connectivity favour a more even distribution with a general downward tendency
- Low action and high connectivity favour pooling around living beings, crystals, and other suitable sites
- High action and high connectivity have no selected distribution behaviour

Animic resistance, cooperative access, and disruption by densely packing an area with static mana-forms remain intended capabilities. Foreign Attuned mana interference is not retained as their present explanation. Their revised mechanisms remain open.

## Deferred Particle Interaction Ideas

These are author suggestions for later comparison, not additional laws:

- High-action particles might be smaller and consequently more permeable
- Low-connectivity particles might be particularly penetrant, inert, or permeable
- Zero Action Potentia might also void Connection Potentia
- Alternatively, zero connectivity might differ from null connectivity, with the former non-permeable and the latter permeable and occurring only at zero Action Potentia

These alternatives leave the relationship between charge, connection capacity, size, and permeability unsettled. Earlier low-energy stable structures remain a possibility, not a guarantee that persists at exactly zero Action Potentia. Detailed dual-potentia interactions and useful particle behaviours require further brainstorming. Most old particle physics remains available for later review, with migration deferred.

## Rejected Package and Deferred Entity

The author discards the remaining universal-noosphere brainstorm from the active design, including lenses, collective reinforcement, the former religious pseudo-agent mechanism, and hand-of-fate ideas. Historical material remains in [[99 Workshop/Magic-System Brainstorms/Earlier Magic-System Proposals#Discarded Noosphere Package|the earlier noosphere account]] for provenance, not as an active alternative.

**Separate deferred proposal:** a conceptual entity residing within the Singularity might provide a new explanation for religious pseudo-agents. Its existence, nature, cognition, and interactions are unselected. It does not restore the discarded collective noosphere.

## Migration and Development Boundary

The previous system will be archived intact before reconstruction. Old mechanics will be considered when their subjects become relevant during new-system writing. Compatibility is not migration approval. No exhaustive keep-or-remove questionnaire is required before the first implementation pass.

The old energy-conservation requirement is not retained for now. Particle conservation and the broad mana cycle are retained at the scope stated above. Detailed recharge, permutation, field interactions, propagation, remote access, material affinities, engineering architecture, and application rules remain deferred rather than silently inherited. Existing deterministic-magic and no-time-travel axioms remain.

Terminology will be isolated as writing progresses, beginning with obvious working concepts such as mana-forms, soul-forms, spell-forms, mana field, mana particles, and attunement. Additional terms require the author's approval of later suggestions. No wholesale terminology copying from the old system is planned. [[99 Workshop/Magic-System Revision#Implementation Sequence|The implementation sequence]] records archival, treatment of external references, and later reconstruction.
