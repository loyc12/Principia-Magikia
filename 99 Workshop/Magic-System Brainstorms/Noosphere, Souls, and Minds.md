## Purpose and Design Commitments

High hardness should coexist with, or be secondary to, a powerful system whose mastery supports recognizably magical casting, mind magic, and imperfect survival beyond bodily death. Magic remains compositional: practitioners assemble learned or understood instructions, with inputs, control, and possible autonomous execution. Concepts identify traits, relations, timing, thresholds, and specified operation chains. They do not invent missing solutions or make a desired outcome executable by itself.

The Arrival-induced renaissance should centre on interoperability, standardized methods, and open sharing. Personal understanding, experience, and specialized tools still distinguish exceptional practitioners. Equipped one-mage armies are allowed in principle, bounded by knowledge, resources, and tools rather than becoming godlike. This supersedes the earlier prohibition. The previous mana-particle architecture is a source of possibilities, not a mandatory foundation for the replacement.

Tool familiarity enables finesse. Disuse, replacement, and dependence on personal quirks can make tools unwieldy, while experience with varied tools improves adaptability. The intended distribution of competence remains pyramidal, with many basic practitioners and few exceptional specialists.

## Minds and Souls

A mind maintains its own cognition, experiences, beliefs, procedures, and representations. It can exist without a soul or noospheric access. An Arrival may acquire a soul only after entering the Expanse. This is a setting premise, not a claim about real-world metaphysics.

A soul develops around a mind as a magical extension. It preserves an imprint of that mind and provides additional capabilities, including initially automatic and potentially conscious access to the noosphere. Its personal references cover only a tiny subset of possible conceptual groupings. Minds and the noosphere have related but non-identical contents, and neither is limited to what the other currently represents.

**Author-set constraint:** souls cannot think on their own. They require a cognitive substrate, biological, galvanic, or potentially magical, to support cognition. A magical cognitive substrate remains optional and deferred. A soul's storage, interface, or imprint must not be treated as an independently thinking person. Formation and continued coherence require a mind or suitable substitute; passive persistence after support is lost remains unresolved.

The author's external-drive, graphics-card, network, and cloud analogy expresses extension of a mind's capacities, not a literal component architecture or permission for substrate-free cognition.

**Intended consequence:** galvanic interactions with forming souls disrupt circuitry enough to make naively built electrocognitive devices, including digital computers designed using Earth physics alone, malfunction or fail. The formation threshold, affected architectures, timing, and mitigation are unselected. This does not yet establish that every electrical circuit forms a soul or that robust computing is impossible.

The relationship between soul and soul-form remains open. A soul might be a particular imprint or organization within a soul-form, or a soul-form might host it. Retaining the useful structural idea does not retain the old mechanism unchanged.

## Universal Noosphere and Personal Concepts

The noosphere is a universal web throughout the Expanse, provisionally conceived without ordinary spatial separation. Its trait nodes and their relationships are not a dictionary of words. What constitutes a trait remains unspecified.

Concepts are overlapping groupings of traits and relationships, referenced differently by individual minds. Their overlap can vary across species, languages, cultures, and individuals. Relations strengthen through co-occurrence in minds, mediated by souls and weighted by an unresolved notion of mental strength or complexity. Instinctive and widely shared patterns, including those used by animals, may consequently be easier to access than rare abstractions.

Private concepts are possible. Prevalent groupings make independent formation, use, and communication easier. Stronger minds are intended to have greater influence and independence, while weaker minds are more susceptible, but neither a single scale of strength nor its biological basis is selected.

Noospheric access chiefly provides loose intuition and generic relational support. Increasingly elaborate combinations become harder to recover coherently from the shared web; the individual must assemble detailed concepts and procedures. Personal representations can nevertheless be precise. Distinguishing similar animals can refine targeting criteria, and distinguishing colours can refine an intended threshold, without conferring unknown facts or automatic execution skill.

Shared associations can include false beliefs. Widely repeated propositions may leave detectable patterns if observation is possible, while isolated personal events are generally lost in the background. The noosphere is neither a truth detector nor an exhaustive memory archive. Exceptional sensitivity may yield vague impressions; live inspection of access and collective cognition remain possibilities, not selected capabilities.

## Lenses and Reciprocal Influence

A personal lens is a possible account of how a soul interprets and accesses the universal web. Shared lenses are optional: repeated access patterns might engrave local routes between physical space and the non-spatial noosphere, biasing nearby souls. Desire paths and pheromone trails are analogies for reinforcement, not selected mechanisms. Culture and language could explain some variation without shared magical lenses.

Possible shared layers could mediate personal access and host religious pseudo-entities. Their nesting, overlap, location, movement, persistence, and relation to communities are unresolved. Local lenses would bias access to one universal web rather than create separate noospheres.

Ordinary reciprocal influence should be slow and subtle. The noosphere chiefly enables conceptual magic; collective belief cannot arbitrarily change the underlying laws. Feedback should remain bounded, but its limiting mechanism is undecided.

Artificial minds and their souls may contribute. Mass production might support deliberate propaganda or conceptual reinforcement. Alternatively, common access through a shared lens could reduce the impact of near-identical minds. No weighting by provenance, duplication, or diversity has been selected.

## Death, Identity, and Apparent Gods

Imperfect preservation, reconstruction, and lichdom should be conceivable without trivializing death. Attempts may preserve knowledge without personality, personality without complete memory, or a mourner's incomplete representation of the deceased. Degradation over time is possible. Active survivors still require cognitive support under the substrate constraint above. Personhood and continuity should remain philosophically debatable rather than settled by an infallible test.

An emergent noospheric influence may subtly affect attention, dreams, interpretation, and social behaviour. The intended hand of fate responds to action and counteraction, with shifting tendencies towards balance rather than convenient solutions. Its nature may remain unexplained or unseen.

Religious figures could be pseudo-agents generated by collective representations, possibly within lenses or through deliberate manipulation. They may seem to communicate concepts or information accessible through the noosphere and weakly influence magic without being independent conscious persons. Their character can drift across generations. Whether any such phenomenon actually thinks is unresolved; true cognition would require a substrate rather than an exception granted to gods.

## Open Implementation Questions

These are unresolved discussion consequences, not additional author-selected mechanisms.

- What substrate activity causes soul formation, how coupling alters that activity, and how biological systems accommodate interference that defeats naive galvanic designs
- What persists without support, what a replacement host must do, and how soul and soul-form should be distinguished
- What traits and relations are, and whether ordered procedures reside in minds, souls, constructs, or some combination rather than emerging from co-occurrence alone
- How concepts connect to perceived subjects without revealing unavailable facts; the distinction between understanding a criterion and detecting its satisfaction
- Whether mental influence, coherence, flexibility, and independence require separate properties rather than a universal strength ranking
- How fast access differs from slow reinforcement, what limits feedback over long periods, and whether deliberate modulation permits communication through a universal web
- Whether lenses exist beyond individuals, how they relate to physical locality, and what makes contributions independent or redundant
- How much live access can be inspected, what prevents unrestricted mental access, and how conceptual detail is lost or retained
- How pseudo-agency arises without unsupported cognition, and what could produce the proposed tendency towards balance

Mago-physical implementation is the next discussion. Mana's role, primitive operations, coupling, costs, access limits, and substrate behaviour remain to be developed. Do not silently import earlier package rankings or prohibitions as binding answers.
