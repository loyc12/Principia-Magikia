**Type: Development working note**

**Status: Exploring**

**Scope: Author-stated direction for soul-forms, personal conceptual webs, and casting, with unresolved mechanisms and optional proposals distinguished below**

## Purpose and Design Commitments

High hardness should coexist with, or be secondary to, a powerful system whose mastery supports recognizably magical casting, mind magic, and imperfect survival beyond bodily death. Magic remains compositional: practitioners assemble learned or understood instructions, with inputs, control, and possible autonomous execution. Concepts identify traits, relations, timing, thresholds, and specified operation chains. They do not invent missing solutions or make a desired outcome executable by itself.

The Arrival-induced renaissance should centre on interoperability, standardized methods, and open sharing. Personal understanding, experience, and specialized tools still distinguish exceptional practitioners. Equipped one-mage armies are allowed in principle, bounded by knowledge, resources, and tools rather than becoming godlike. This supersedes the earlier prohibition. The previous mana-particle architecture is a source of possibilities, not a mandatory foundation for the replacement.

Tool familiarity enables finesse. Disuse, replacement, and dependence on personal quirks can make tools unwieldy, while experience with varied tools improves adaptability. The intended distribution of competence remains pyramidal, with many basic practitioners and few exceptional specialists.

## Minds and Soul-Forms

A mind maintains its own cognition, experiences, beliefs, procedures, and representations. It can exist without a soul-form. An Arrival may acquire one only after entering the Expanse. This is a setting premise, not a claim about real-world metaphysics.

**Author-selected direction:** soul-forms are a subtype of mana-form that occurs naturally around a cognitive substrate. They record conceptual occurrences and relationships found in the host substrate's ongoing operation. Once properly trained, the host can manipulate these conceptual patterns to enact magical effects through mana manipulation. The process connecting conceptual manipulation to mana behaviour remains unresolved.

The umbrella term mana-form includes soul-forms and spell-forms. The new particle foundation does not retain the old architecture wholesale. The exact distinction between soul and soul-form remains open pending terminology review.

**Author-set constraint:** souls cannot think independently of a cognitive substrate. That substrate may be biological, galvanic, or potentially magical. A magical cognitive substrate remains optional and deferred. A soul-form's recorded patterns and capacity to support casting do not establish an independently thinking person. Natural formation requires cognitive activity; continued coherence, substitute support, and passive persistence after the host is lost remain unresolved.

The earlier network and cloud analogies belong to the discarded noosphere package. External memory and a mana-based analogue of a graphics processor are renewed application ideas for autonomous conceptual organization, described below, rather than selected architectures.

**Intended consequence:** galvanic interactions with forming soul-forms disrupt circuitry enough to make naively built electrocognitive devices, including digital computers designed using Earth physics alone, malfunction or fail. The formation threshold, affected architectures, timing, and mitigation are unselected. This does not establish that every electrical circuit forms a soul-form or that robust computing is impossible.

## Conceptual Webs and Conceptual Chains

**Conceptual web** is the working term for the personal concepts and their organization recorded in a soul-form. It reflects conceptual occurrences and relationships in the host's operation. It is not a lens onto a universal reference system. The precise relation between representations in the mind and recorded patterns in the soul-form remains unresolved; recording does not establish a complete or exact autobiographical archive.

**Conceptual chains** are selected paths or sets of paths within a conceptual web, used to enact casting. A chain need not be a single linear sequence. How the host selects, manipulates, and executes chains, and how their organization directs mana, remain implementation questions.

Personal concepts and relationships can differ across individuals, species, languages, cultures, and experience. Personal representations can nevertheless be precise: distinguishing similar animals can refine a targeting criterion, and distinguishing colours can refine a threshold. Neither supplies unavailable observations, missing knowledge, or automatic execution skill. Understanding a criterion remains distinct from detecting whether a subject satisfies it.

The universal catalogue describes the fundamental conceptual nodes allowed by the system's laws, not a separate space or shared memory. Personal webs organize those building blocks through individual experience. The remaining noosphere package, including lenses and collective reinforcement, is discarded from the active design.

The strengthened mind–soul-form bond supports a trainable additional mana sense, potentially at finer levels, and refinement of mana and conceptual manipulation. Its exact resolution and coupling mechanism remain open.

## Autonomous Conceptual Webs

**Author-selected development direction:** conceptual webs may operate autonomously and modify their own organization. Such pseudo-minds may exist temporarily without a material body while still requiring a soul-form or equivalent mana-form subject to degradation. They are not established as true minds or conscious persons. The cognitive-substrate constraint must not be used to prohibit this adaptive behaviour merely because it occurs in mana.

**Possible applications, not implemented capabilities:** lichdom, apparently sentient spells or wards, soul magic, mind manipulation, external memory, and externalized thought processes. The author's mana-based graphics-processor analogy describes an auxiliary structure connected to a mind to augment its processing. How it connects, what it can do, and how it differs from a true mind remain open.

Self-modification, persistence, and autonomy require later mechanisms. The separate static/dynamic assembly proposal and its possible coupling to neuronal or galvanic flows are deferred in [[99 Workshop/Magic-System Brainstorms/Mana and Conceptual Foundations|Mana and Conceptual Foundations]]. Research into a genuinely cognitive magical substrate remains optional and deferred.

## Unstructured and Structured Casting

### Unstructured Casting

Unstructured casting operates directly through the practitioner's soul-form by manipulating its internally held conceptual patterns and chains. It draws on the practitioner's ongoing cognition and learned procedures without requiring the creation of a separate spell-form. The term does not mean that the conceptual web lacks organization or that intent supplies missing procedures.

### Structured Casting

Structured casting requires, by definition, the creation of a **spell-form**: an artificial analogue of a soul-form, adapted to exist independently of a cognitive substrate. The changes that make this independence possible remain unexplained. Temporary or persistent powering is the author's tentative expectation, not a selected maintenance mechanism.

A spell-form receives duplicated conceptual patterns from a soul-form or another spell-form. It must carry enough organization to perform its intended task with the inputs and support available to it. Externalization need not be a straightforward copy of everything the practitioner supplies during unstructured casting. How patterns are transferred, adapted, combined, and made executable remains open.

Independent existence and autonomous self-modification do not establish true cognition or personhood. Spell execution, retained state, adaptive conceptual webs, and consciousness remain distinct questions. Their mechanisms and limits have not been fully specified.

**Optional author proposal:** static spell-forms could serve as libraries of predefined, inert concept-based snippets of magical programs, providing patterns for duplication into other spell-forms. Whether such libraries are possible, how inert patterns are maintained, and what activates a copied pattern remain unresolved. Inert does not yet mean self-sustaining or free of maintenance costs.

## Death and Identity

Imperfect preservation, reconstruction, and lichdom should be conceivable without trivializing death. Attempts may preserve knowledge without personality, personality without complete memory, or a mourner's incomplete representation of the deceased. Degradation over time is possible. A survivor claimed to be a true mind still requires cognitive support under the substrate constraint above. An autonomous conceptual remnant may instead exhibit pseudo-mind behaviour without that claim. Personhood and continuity should remain philosophically debatable rather than settled by an infallible test.

These aims remain active without requiring a universal noosphere. How conceptual webs persist, degrade, or transfer after loss of the original host remains unresolved.

## Open Implementation Questions

These questions identify unfinished mechanisms, not additional selected capabilities.

- What substrate activity produces a soul-form, how recording and coupling occur, and how biological systems accommodate interference that defeats naive galvanic designs
- How the conceptual web relates to ongoing host cognition, what is retained, and how practice changes access to and manipulation of recorded patterns
- What constitutes a concept or relationship, how chains are selected and combined, and what turns their manipulation into mana operations
- How casting obtains observations, selects subjects, and controls effects without treating conceptual recognition as unrestricted detection or access
- What modifications allow a spell-form to exist independently of a cognitive substrate, and what temporary or persistent support it needs
- How concepts and chains can be duplicated between forms, what context must accompany them, and what limits fidelity or interoperability
- Whether inert spell-form libraries are possible and how storage, activation, execution, feedback, and optional learning differ
- What persists after a host or power supply is lost, what a replacement host must provide, and what separates an imprint from supported active cognition
- How mental access and resistance work, and whether influence, coherence, flexibility, and independence require distinct properties rather than one strength ranking

## Distinctions to Carry into Mana Design

These are assistant-identified questions and suggestions retained for the next brainstorm, not additional author-selected mechanics. They can be addressed during mana design rather than treated as prerequisites for another use-case design round.

- **Association and execution:** recorded conceptual relationships do not yet explain sequence, conditions, repetition, or stopping; determine whether this organization comes from learned host procedures, deliberate assembly during casting, or both, and how mana executes it
- **Cognitive independence and support:** a spell-form may exist without a cognitive substrate while still requiring power, an anchor, maintenance, or supervision; independent existence does not establish permanence, autonomy, or self-sustenance, and inert storage may require different support from execution
- **Context and portability:** copied concepts such as “my hand,” “upright,” or “warm enough” may depend on their original host's references and observations; establish what must accompany or replace that context during transfer; allowing both personally calibrated and deliberately portable patterns is an unselected suggestion that could give standardization a concrete purpose

## Deferred Terminology Review

Conceptual web, conceptual chain, unstructured casting, structured casting, mana-form, soul-form, and spell-form are working terms. Terminology will be isolated as the replacement is written, with additional suggestions submitted for author approval. The relationships among soul, form, shape, pattern, formula, structure, construct, and related vocabulary remain to be clarified where relevant. The present hyphenation follows local usage without settling that review. Preserve the old system intact for archival rather than propagating terminology changes into it.

The selected mana foundation and its explicit deferrals are recorded in [[99 Workshop/Magic-System Brainstorms/Mana and Conceptual Foundations|Mana and Conceptual Foundations]]. Conceptual manipulation and execution should be prioritized after the base system is established, including how soul-forms or spell-forms are guided by minds or by conceptual organization acting upon other concepts. Do not silently import earlier package rankings or prohibitions as binding answers.
