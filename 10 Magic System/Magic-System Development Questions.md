# Magic-System Development Questions

**Type: Development questions**

**Status: Exploring**

**Development: Deferred**

**Approval: Required before adoption into the references**

This file records selected future developments that have not yet been adopted into the numbered references.

## Deferred Development

### Lightning-Born Coherence

Natural lightning may abruptly alter electrical, material, and mana conditions sufficiently to nucleate many short-lived Primed microstructures. It does not create mana. Most resulting structures should collapse rapidly. Rare survivors may be stabilized or harvested only where ambient Primed concentration, material context, and preparation are favourable.

Before adopting this in the reference, decide its observable consequences: whether it leaves a detectable residue, whether it increases wild Attunement or counterreactions on collapse, how often it produces useful material sites, and whether Galvanic magic can safely imitate any part of the process.

### Chirality and Keys

Without intrinsic particle orientation, chirality belongs to the relation between a scaffold and a declared reference geometry: a physical anchor, gravity, a body, a material frame, or a larger asymmetric construct. Mirror-image motifs are otherwise equivalent.

With an admissible reference, a sequence of left- and right-handed simple positional motifs could serve as a difficult-to-forge geometric key. Later work should decide whether such keys are merely recognized by a construct's Sensing and Processing components or whether particular attributes receive specialized uses from chirality.

### Quantitative Treatment

If useful later, [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/10 Mana Physics|Mana Physics]] may model coherence-well depth, formation and escape barriers, local concentration, coherence capacity, and interference. Do not introduce detailed equations before deciding which of these are measurable and which remain qualitative engineering concepts.

### Scaffold Form and Function

The relationship between a scaffold's positional arrangement and its engineering capabilities remains undeveloped. A later model might assign characteristic roles to paths, junctions, shell-like regions, or lattices, but no geometric element currently has an inherent canonical function. Until that model exists, declaration, components, interfaces, and available mana define construct behaviour. Scaffold form alone does not.

### Hand Motions and Somatic Control
Consider hand motions for manipulating spellcasting, since soul-form interactions are required both to construct spells and to keep their constructs in place.
