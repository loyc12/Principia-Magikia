# Spell-Forms

**Type: Development stub**

**Approval: Retains the source brainstorm’s selection scope; tentative ideas and open questions are not adopted mechanics**

**Development: Notes for terminology selection and later detailed documentation**

## Structured Casting Notes

Intended applications include wards, tools, engines, autonomous constructs, and golems. Their detailed architectures remain undeveloped.

Structured casting requires, by definition, the creation of a **spell-form**: an artificial analogue of a soul-form, adapted to exist independently of a cognitive substrate. The changes that make this independence possible remain unexplained. Temporary or persistent powering is the author's tentative expectation, not a selected maintenance mechanism.

A spell-form receives duplicated conceptual patterns from a soul-form or another spell-form. It must carry enough organization to perform its intended task with the inputs and support available to it. Externalization need not be a straightforward copy of everything the practitioner supplies during unstructured casting. How patterns are transferred, adapted, combined, and made executable remains open.

Independent existence and autonomous self-modification do not establish true cognition or personhood. Spell execution, retained state, adaptive conceptual webs, and consciousness remain distinct questions. Their mechanisms and limits have not been fully specified.

**Optional author proposal:** static spell-forms could serve as libraries of predefined, inert concept-based snippets of magical programs, providing patterns for duplication into other spell-forms. Whether such libraries are possible, how inert patterns are maintained, and what activates a copied pattern remain unresolved. Inert does not yet mean self-sustaining or free of maintenance costs.

## Open Implementation Questions

- What modifications allow a spell-form to exist independently of a cognitive substrate, and what temporary or persistent support it needs
- How concepts and chains can be duplicated between forms, what context must accompany them, and what limits fidelity or interoperability
- Whether inert spell-form libraries are possible and how storage, activation, execution, feedback, and optional learning differ

## Support and Portability

These are assistant-identified questions and suggestions, not additional selected mechanics.

- **Cognitive independence and support:** a spell-form may exist without a cognitive substrate while still requiring power, an anchor, maintenance, or supervision; independent existence does not establish permanence, autonomy, or self-sustenance, and inert storage may require different support from execution
- **Context and portability:** copied concepts such as “my hand,” “upright,” or “warm enough” may depend on their original host's references and observations; establish what must accompany or replace that context during transfer; allowing both personally calibrated and deliberately portable patterns is an unselected suggestion that could give standardization a concrete purpose
