# Magical Access and Protection

**Type: Development stub**

**Status: Exploring**

**Scope: Resistance, cooperation, disruption, and unresolved access mechanisms**

**Approval: Retains the source brainstorm’s selection scope; tentative ideas and open questions are not adopted mechanics**

## Intended Capabilities

Animic resistance, cooperative access, and disruption by densely packing an area with static mana-forms remain intended capabilities. Foreign Attuned mana interference is not retained as their present explanation. Their revised mechanisms remain open.

Conceptual recognition does not provide unrestricted detection or access. Observing, locating, connecting to, interpreting, and altering a subject remain distinct requirements.

## Open Implementation Questions

- How mental access and resistance work, and whether influence, coherence, flexibility, and independence require distinct properties rather than one strength ranking
- Establish how observations and subject selection work in [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/07 Mana Casting|Mana Casting]]
- Establish remote access, propagation, cooperative permission, and the costs of protection

## Historical Leads for Reassessment

The earlier proposals retain mind-magic preferences, prepared endpoints, cords, selective channels, field geometry, and magical-distance alternatives in [[99 Workshop/Magic-System Brainstorms/Earlier Magic-System Proposals#Leads for Later Reassessment|their historical owner]]. They remain unselected leads rather than active mechanisms.
