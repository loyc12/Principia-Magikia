# Soul-Forms and Minds

**Type: Development stub**

**Status: Exploring**

**Scope: Mind coupling, substrate-dependent cognition, mana sense, and galvanic disruption**

**Approval: Retains the source brainstorm’s selection scope; tentative ideas and open questions are not adopted mechanics**

**Development: Notes for terminology selection and later detailed documentation**

## Selected Direction and Constraints

A mind maintains its own cognition, experiences, beliefs, procedures, and representations. It can exist without a soul-form. An Arrival may acquire one only after entering the Expanse. This is a setting premise, not a claim about real-world metaphysics.

**Author-selected direction:** soul-forms are a subtype of mana-form that occurs naturally around a cognitive substrate. They record conceptual occurrences and relationships found in the host substrate's ongoing operation. Once properly trained, the host can manipulate these conceptual patterns to enact magical effects through mana manipulation. The process connecting conceptual manipulation to mana behaviour remains unresolved.

The umbrella term mana-form includes soul-forms and spell-forms. The new particle foundation does not retain the old architecture wholesale. The exact distinction between soul and soul-form remains open pending terminology review.

**Author-set constraint:** souls cannot think independently of a cognitive substrate. That substrate may be biological, galvanic, or potentially magical. A magical cognitive substrate remains optional and deferred. A soul-form's recorded patterns and capacity to support casting do not establish an independently thinking person. Natural formation requires cognitive activity; continued coherence, substitute support, and passive persistence after the host is lost remain unresolved.

**Intended consequence:** galvanic interactions with forming soul-forms disrupt circuitry enough to make naively built electrocognitive devices, including digital computers designed using Earth physics alone, malfunction or fail. The formation threshold, affected architectures, timing, and mitigation are unselected. This does not establish that every electrical circuit forms a soul-form or that robust computing is impossible.

The strengthened mind–soul-form bond supports a trainable additional mana sense, potentially at finer levels, and refinement of mana and conceptual manipulation. Its exact resolution and coupling mechanism remain open.

## Open Implementation Questions

- What substrate activity produces a soul-form, how recording and coupling occur, and how biological systems accommodate interference that defeats naive galvanic designs
- How the conceptual web relates to ongoing host cognition, what is retained, and how practice changes access to and manipulation of recorded patterns
