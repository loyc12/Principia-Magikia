## Fundamental Nodes

The former phrase **conceptual space** now means the universal catalogue of possible fundamental conceptual nodes permitted by the system's laws. It is not an additional place or an inhabited repository. The author's comparisons are the IPA's inventory of phonemes and the periodic table's description of permitted atomic forms.

Fundamental nodes need not correspond to familiar whole concepts. They may be esoteric building blocks from which personal concepts are assembled. **Fly** is composite. As illustrative decompositions rather than selected nodes, **up** might combine direction, away, and base, or direction, near, and centre. The actual node inventory has not been designed.

## Personal Webs and Chains

**Conceptual web** is the working term for the personal concepts and their organization recorded in a soul-form. It reflects conceptual occurrences and relationships in the host's operation. It is not a lens onto a universal reference system. The precise relation between representations in the mind and recorded patterns in the soul-form remains unresolved; recording does not establish a complete or exact autobiographical archive.

**Conceptual chains** are selected paths or sets of paths within a conceptual web, used to enact casting. A chain need not be a single linear sequence. How the host selects, manipulates, and executes chains, and how their organization directs mana, remain implementation questions.

Personal concepts and relationships can differ across individuals, species, languages, cultures, and experience. Personal representations can nevertheless be precise: distinguishing similar animals can refine a targeting criterion, and distinguishing colours can refine a threshold. Neither supplies unavailable observations, missing knowledge, or automatic execution skill. Understanding a criterion remains distinct from detecting whether a subject satisfies it.

The universal catalogue describes the fundamental conceptual nodes allowed by the system's laws, not a separate space or shared memory. Personal webs organize those building blocks through individual experience. The remaining noosphere package, including lenses and collective reinforcement, is discarded from the active design.

## Execution and Attunement Notes

Personal conceptual webs organize these building blocks through the host's experience and understanding. Conceptual chains combine concepts, criteria, and procedures to enact magic. Shared fundamental nodes do not supply missing procedures, observations, or personal context.

The intended practice resembles low-level programming: practitioners organize learned concepts and criteria into executable chains rather than engineering every effect through detailed particle physics. The mana field may provide the mechanism connecting conceptual organization, attunement, and effects. The earlier suggestion that nearby concept-associated sources naturally attune particles remains tentative. Birds producing flow-, movement-, or flight-associated mana were exploratory examples, not adopted attunements or a selected detection mechanism. Both natural and artificial attunement mechanisms are deferred, including whether conceptual proximity is used at all.
