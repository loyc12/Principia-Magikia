## Selected Relationships

**Mana-forms** is the working umbrella term for coherent mana assemblies, including soul-forms and spell-forms. Particles may flow freely or assemble through their connection capacity. Natural assembly occurs where sufficient mana concentration and connection-enabling conditions coincide. Living beings, particularly active neurological systems, provide such conditions for soul-form formation. Other sources remain possible. Coherent assembly alone does not establish a soul-form or consciousness.

[[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/05 Soul-Forms and Minds|Soul-Forms and Minds]] owns natural cognitive coupling; [[10 Magic System/01 Key Concepts/08 Spell-Forms|Spell-Forms]] owns artificial spell-forms. Coherent assembly alone establishes neither cognition nor casting.

## Tentative Assembly Distinction

**Tentative assembly distinction:** static assemblies could resemble rigid objects, while dynamic assemblies could combine a firmer portion with a flexible, soup-like portion responsive to concepts and neuronal or galvanic activity. The distinction and its mechanisms remain deferred.
