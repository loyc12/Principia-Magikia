# Mana Circulation

**Type: Development stub**

**Status: Exploring**

**Scope: Broad circulation, tentative distribution, and the separate Singularity proposal**

**Approval: Retains the source brainstorm’s selection scope; tentative ideas and open questions are not adopted mechanics**

**Development: Notes for terminology selection and later detailed documentation**

## Broad Cycle

The broad mana cycle is retained. For now, **Spent mana goes up towards the Singularity; charged mana goes down** is the working rule of thumb. Primed and Spent remain preferred labels for maximal and minimal charge, but two potentia properties require later clarification of their coverage. The old permutation package is not migrated by retaining these labels.

The following distribution ideas are tentative and deferred:

- Low Action Potentia and low Connection Potentia correspond to Spent mana moving towards the Singularity
- High action and low connectivity favour a more even distribution with a general downward tendency
- Low action and high connectivity favour pooling around living beings, crystals, and other suitable sites
- High action and high connectivity have no selected distribution behaviour

## Separate Deferred Entity

**Separate deferred proposal:** a conceptual entity residing within the Singularity might provide a new explanation for religious pseudo-agents. Its existence, nature, cognition, and interactions are unselected. It does not restore the discarded collective noosphere.
