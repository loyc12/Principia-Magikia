
**NOTES :**

*A minimal execution cycle might be:*

1. ***Acquire***  
    *Sourcing components draw or accept mana.*
2. ***Validate\**** 
    *The construct checks mana state, attribute compatibility, reagent eligibility, and activation conditions.*
3. ***Attune\****  
    *Primed mana is converted where required.*
4. ***Address***  
    *The reagent and affected region are resolved.*
5. ***Parameterise***  
    *Magnitude, direction, rate, duration, and constraints are fixed.*
6. ***Apply***  
    *Attuned mana performs its primitive operation upon the reagent.*
7. ***Observe\****  
    *Sensors inspect relevant state changes.*
8. ***Regulate***  
    *Feedback modifies flow or terminates execution.*
9. ***Discharge***  
    *Used mana becomes Spent mana; excess is routed away.*
10. ***Terminate***  
    *Links are released and the scaffold dissipates or returns to dormancy.*
