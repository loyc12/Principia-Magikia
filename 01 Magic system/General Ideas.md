# Magic-System Ideas

This file records selected future developments that have not yet been adopted into the numbered references.

## Deferred Development

### Lightning-Born Coherence

Natural lightning may abruptly alter electrical, material, and mana conditions sufficiently to nucleate many short-lived Primed microstructures. It does not create mana. Most resulting structures should collapse rapidly; rare survivors may be stabilised or harvested only where ambient Primed concentration, material context, and preparation are favourable.

Before adopting this in the reference, decide its observable consequences: whether it leaves a detectable residue, whether it increases wild Attunement or counterreactions on collapse, how often it produces useful material sites, and whether Galvanic magic can safely imitate any part of the process.

### Chirality and Keys

Without intrinsic particle orientation, chirality belongs to the relation between a scaffold and a declared reference geometry: a physical anchor, gravity, a body, a material frame, or a larger asymmetric construct. Mirror-image motifs are otherwise equivalent.

With an admissible reference, a sequence of left- and right-handed simple positional motifs could serve as a difficult-to-forge geometric key. Later work should decide whether such keys are merely recognised by a construct's Sensing and Processing components or whether particular attributes receive specialised uses from chirality.

### Quantitative Treatment

If useful later, [[01 Key Concepts/10 Mana Physics|Mana Physics]] may model coherence-well depth, formation and escape barriers, local concentration, coherence capacity, and interference. Do not introduce detailed equations before deciding which of these are measurable and which remain qualitative engineering concepts.

### Scaffold Form and Function

The relationship between a scaffold's positional arrangement and its engineering capabilities remains undeveloped. A later model might assign characteristic roles to paths, junctions, shell-like regions, or lattices, but no geometric element currently has an inherent canonical function. Until that model exists, declaration, components, interfaces, and available mana—not scaffold form alone—define construct behaviour.
