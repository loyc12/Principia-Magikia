**Wild Casting** ( sometimes referred to as *instinctive casting* ) is the act of casting a spell without the use of a symbolic spell construct. It is usually structurally crude and inflexible, but may be highly efficient within the exact use-case for which the organ evolved. It becomes inefficient when forced outside its native operating conditions.

Wild casting primarily serves to create basic effects, and is most often seen in creatures, but some plants are also documented as being able to cast spells. There are also rumours that members of certain [[sapient species]] ( primarily children ) are capable of wild casting, but these remain unconfirmed.

The leading theory proposes that dedicated organs fulfil some or all of the functions normally performed by mana constructs, along with an instinctive mental ability to funnel mana into this pseudo-construct organ. Dissections of these suspected organs has yet to reveal any symbolic patterns, suggesting that wild casting does not depend on physical symbolic patterning.

As such, wild casting would only be possible for beings endowed with these body parts, and the instinctual understanding of mana manipulation. This would explain why magical creatures may be able to cast simple spells despite lacking sapience, or sometimes even sentience altogether.

## Strengths :

- incredibly fast at casting
- can be performed under stress
- integrated with anatomy

## Weaknesses :

- narrow effect range
- impossible to alter its outcome
- inefficient outside evolved use-case


NOTES :

Wild-casting organs contain no symbolic notation because their physical and biochemical organisation directly generates a fixed scaffold and declaration.

The evolutionary constraints naturally explain:

- narrow effect range;
- high speed;
- excellent stress performance;
- strong reagent specificity;
- low conscious flexibility.

It would also produce interesting fauna:

- organisms whose organs slowly accumulate Attuned mana;
- predators with one extremely efficient discharge spell;
- plants with continuous low-power constructs;
- creatures whose spells are partly powered by environmental mana flow;
- organs that use biological tissue as scaffold stabilisers.

The claim that wild casting is limited to beings possessing dedicated organs may be too absolute. Some organisms might use:

- distributed tissues;
- crystalline inclusions;
- colony-level structures;
- symbiotic organisms;
- temporary developmental organs.

Those variations would broaden magical biology without abandoning the premise.
