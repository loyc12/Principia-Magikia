NOTES :

The glyph system is good provided glyphs remain representational. Different systems can differ in:

- granularity;
- notation order;
- abstraction level;
- whether they depict topology or behaviour;
- whether they favour declarative or procedural descriptions.

This gives your protagonist room to realise that traditional glyphs are not magic itself but merely poorly standardised programming languages, circuit diagrams, or mathematical notations.
