The various ways spells can be used in a practical setting :

- cantrips;
- enchanting;
- implements;
- focuses;
- wards;
- engines;
- arrays.


NOTES : 

Possible disciplines include:

- **construct composition**: arranging known components;
- **parameterisation**: altering a known construct safely;
- **refactoring**: replacing components while preserving output;
- **derivation**: adapting an existing formula to a new reagent;
- **original design**: producing a genuinely novel construct;
- **live shaping**: altering an active construct during execution;
- **diagnostics**: inspecting and repairing malformed constructs.
