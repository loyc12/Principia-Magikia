# Biggest Weakness

The protagonist thinks **too much** and experiences **too little**.

Across six days we receive dozens of paragraphs of speculation.

We receive comparatively few actual events.

Imagine if instead we had:

Day 3

- lost

Day 4

- monster

Day 5

- magical phenomenon

Day 6

- near drowning

Day 7

- hunter

Day 8

- mysterious ruins

Same amount of narration,

but each day revolves around a memorable external event.

Currently several days are mostly internal monologue.

# Biggest Strength

Consistency.

The voice never slips.

The protagonist always sounds like the same person.

His reasoning style stays coherent.

The journal format feels authentic.

The humour arises naturally from personality.

That's difficult to maintain over nearly 5,000 words, and you've done it well.
