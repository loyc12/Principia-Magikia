# Character Archetypes

|Character|Represents|
|---|---|
|Protagonist|Curiosity, rational inquiry, optimism|
|Golem|Emergent personhood|
|Archivist|Careful scholarship|
|Court Mage|Knowledge as political power|
|Village Healer|Practical compassion|
|Merchant|Innovation through commerce|
|Priest|Wisdom and humility|
|Peer Researcher|Intellectual rivalry|
|Conservative Scholar|Stability and tradition|
|Student|Future generations|

## Character 1 — The Archivist

Belief : Knowledge belongs to humanity.

Almost identical to the protagonist.

Difference : Knowledge must first be verified.

They are cautious, the protagonist is impatient.


## Character 2 — The Court Mage

Belief : Knowledge is political power.

Publishing everything leads to chaos.
History has taught this lesson repeatedly.

Not evil, just cynical.


## Character 3 — The Village Healer

Belief : Theory is less important than helping people.

They don't care how magic works.
They care whether children survive winter.
They continually remind the protagonist :

 "Congratulations on your paper. The harvest still failed."


## Character 4 — The Merchant

Belief : Ideas are valuable because they make money.

They are fascinated by magical industry.

They finances research, but only profitable research.


## Character 5 — The Priest

Belief : Magic should cultivate wisdom, not power.

They aren't anti-science.

They're anti-arrogance.

Sometimes they're right, sometimes not.


## Character 6 — The Golem

Its beliefs don't start fully formed.

Initially, it simply reflects the protagonist.

Gradually...

It develops its own philosophy.

Eventually it disagrees.

That's when the protagonist realises:

"I didn't create a tool, I created another mind."
