# Protagonist Arc

|Stage|Internal Goal|
|---|---|
|Arrival|Return home|
|Survival|Stay alive|
|Isolation|Find companionship|
|Integration|Become accepted|
|Discovery|Understand magic|
|Application|Improve lives|
|Reform|Change magical scholarship|
|Legacy|Build institutions that outlast him|
