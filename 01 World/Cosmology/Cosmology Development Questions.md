# Cosmology Development Questions

**Type: Development questions**

**Status: Exploring**

## Surface

Concrete additions for later:

- **The Settlement Belts**
    Define each habitable band’s typical temperature, rainfall, dominant vegetation, and seasonal risk. For example, specify whether its poleward edge is drought-prone and its equatorial edge is storm-prone.

- **Surface Waters**
    Explain whether seas follow gravitational equipotentials, how rivers and aquifers drain outward through the shell, and how often labyrinths form beneath large lakes or coastal basins.

- **Labyrinths**
    Establish whether they are entrance-cave regions, collapsed cave systems, or distinct mana-shaped formations. Include their usual hazards, ecology, and importance to trade and mining.

- **The Third-Stratum Thermal Inversion**
    Give scholars’ leading explanation for the 45 °C upper layer and the 3 km mana peak, perhaps a local interaction between dense mana, water circulation, and volcanic activity.

- **Recorded Depths**
    Add a short table of known depth records, expeditions, mortality, equipment requirements, and what remains unknown beyond the Fourth Stratum.
