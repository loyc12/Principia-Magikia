# Scientific Philosophy

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Current foundation**

The protagonist does not introduce thought, observation, literacy, or practical expertise to an ignorant world. Existing practitioners may be excellent observers, careful craftworkers, and capable teachers, with generations of useful empirical knowledge.

The difference is the degree to which methods are made explicit, comparable, and public. The protagonist's contribution is to connect practices that may already exist in limited forms into a self-correcting system of measurement, controlled testing, recorded failure, replication, theory, and organized dissemination.

| Existing tendency | Reforming tendency |
| --- | --- |
| Apprenticeship and guarded practice | Documentation that can be inspected beyond one lineage |
| Local or inherited units | Calibrated measurements and stated uncertainty |
| Skilled observation | Controlled comparison and explicit hypotheses |
| Authority, reputation, and precedent | Replication and review across independent groups |
| Restricted records | Deliberate circulation of appropriate results |
| Individual mastery | Shared methods, instruments, and teaching |
| Trial, error, and remembered successes | Recorded failures and formal models |

The societal reasons these tendencies persist belong in [[Knowledge Systems|Knowledge Systems]].
