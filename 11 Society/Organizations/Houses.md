# Houses

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Current foundation**

**Houses** are enduring political-professional lineages built around accumulated capability rather than blood alone. A House may hold archives, property, workshops, instruments, contracts, patronage ties, teachers, and specialized methods. It can therefore resemble an extended family, a school, a firm, a noble household, and a local political faction without being directly equivalent to any one of them.

## Membership and Continuity

Membership may be inherited, invited, adopted, earned through service, transferred, resigned, or revoked. Biological kinship remains influential because it supplies early trust and continuity, but it is not sufficient to explain a House's survival. A House must also retain and attract talented people, useful resources, private knowledge, and a recognized claim to its name.

Questions for later development:

- What does membership obligate a person to provide, and what protection or education does it return?
- Who can admit, expel, inherit from, or divide a House?
- How do Houses preserve useful knowledge while avoiding loss through secrecy, disaster, or defection?
- When does a House owe service to a city, ruler, league, or larger House, and when can it act independently?

## Political Role

Houses can become vassals, patrons, officeholders, landholders, local governors, or sovereign powers because trained magi and their supporting resources are politically valuable. Their authority must nevertheless rest on practical dependencies: food, labour, protection, legitimacy, supply, and the willingness of others to recognize their claims.

This makes Houses one recurring element of [[11 Society/Politics/Political Power|political power]], not a replacement for every other institution.
