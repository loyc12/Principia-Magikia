# Everyday Life

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Current foundation**

Instead of importing an assumed historical baseline, ask:

- What does magic make easier?
- What remains difficult?
- Who owns, maintains, and can afford the useful magical work?
- Which ordinary problems still depend on skilled labour, materials, transport, or seasonal conditions?

Magic can make one part of a task easy while creating a new dependency on instruments, mana supply, trained operators, maintenance, or legal permission. The relevant balance is developed in [[11 Society/Economy/Magical Development|Magical Development]].

These choices shape homes, work, class, and the physical form of the first settlement.
