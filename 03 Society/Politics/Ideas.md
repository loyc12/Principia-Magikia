# Society

## Knowledge networks.

[[02 Mana Physics|Magic]] requires teachers.
Teachers require apprentices.
Apprentices become teachers.

The result is overlapping institutional webs.

Political borders matter less than scholarly lineages.

This also gives the protagonist a natural route into society.

# Rulers

Rather than asking : Who rules?

Ask : Why do people obey?

Maybe:

- magical expertise
- military force
- religion
- wealth
- bureaucracy
- historical legitimacy

Usually several simultaneously.

# The relationship between rulers and ruled

This doesn't have to resemble medieval Europe.

Perhaps rulers exist primarily because large magical infrastructure needs coordination.

Think irrigation, except magical.

Now governance feels functional and purposeful.
