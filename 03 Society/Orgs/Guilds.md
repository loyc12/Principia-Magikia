# Guilds

Guilds are professional associations formed to solve recurring problems in a trade or practice. Their structure and authority vary by place, profession, and political settlement; a guild need not be a uniform, realm-wide organisation.

## Problems They Can Solve

- train apprentices when teaching is slow and experienced workers risk losing their investment
- certify competence where buyers cannot readily judge quality or safety
- establish shared measures, contracts, inspection, and dispute procedures
- coordinate access to scarce tools, sites, materials, transport, or information
- bargain collectively with patrons, Houses, governments, and merchants
- preserve a profession's methods while deciding what can safely be shared

Magic can intensify these pressures when poor work damages people, property, mana supplies, or durable infrastructure. It can also create grounds for conflict with [[Houses|Houses]], colleges, and governments over licensing, instruction, safety rules, and proprietary methods.

## Boundaries

Guilds are not general-purpose adventurer handlers. Delvers, mercenaries, carriers, guides, and odd-job workers may organise themselves where their work creates a real need for coordination, insurance, reputation, or mutual protection, but each should be treated as its own occupation.

Questions for a local guild:

- Whose risk or information problem makes this guild valuable?
- Which powers are legally granted, commercially tolerated, or merely enforced through reputation?
- Who is excluded, and what alternative path exists outside the guild?
- What does the guild need from local authorities in return for its privileges?
