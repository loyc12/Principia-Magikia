# Culture

Different societies solve the same problems with different "design philosophies."

For example:

- Village A prioritises robustness
- Village B prioritises efficiency
- City C prioritises modular specialisation
- Empire D prioritises centralised optimisation

Those aren't just political choices, they influence :

- Architecture
- Trade
- Education
- Magic
- Law
- Family structures
- Etc

Culture is not reducible to species. Shared languages, mixed institutions, migration, class, trade, local ecology, and the practical accommodation of different bodies all produce cultural variation. The local linguistic consequences of this are collected in [[Languages|Languages]].
