# Languages

The Expanse's languages reflect Arrival history, migration, mixed communities, trade, prestige, assimilation, and the loss of older speech communities. Species and language do not map one-to-one.

## Local Shared Speech

Where several species live and work together, one or more trade language or creole can develop around sounds and structures that its regular speakers can reliably produce and perceive. This shared speech permits daily cooperation without erasing native languages.

It may be limited in formal precision, emotional nuance, ceremonial use, or technical vocabulary. Speakers can therefore have sharply different access to law, instruction, magical education, and social intimacy even when they appear conversationally fluent.

## Design Questions

- Which local species can produce, hear, and distinguish which sound classes?
- Who grows up bilingual, who learns a shared speech for work, and who depends on interpreters?
- Which language carries literacy, official authority, trade, religious practice, or House membership?
- What misunderstanding does the protagonist make by treating a shared speech as culturally neutral?
