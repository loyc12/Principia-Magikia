# Protagonist

**Type: Narrative planning**

**Status: Current direction**

## Motivations

|Motivation|Narrative Role|
|---|---|
|Curiosity|Drives exploration and experimentation|
|Need to Belong|Pushes social integration|
|Loneliness|Leads to creating the golem|
|Teaching|Motivates publication and dissemination|
|Indignation|Drives challenges to institutions and misconceptions|
|Financial Necessity|Keeps discoveries grounded in practical work|
|Legacy|Eventually motivates building institutions rather than merely making discoveries|

|Teaching|Students and mentorship|
|Patronage|Politics and obligation|
|Exploration|Discovery of magical resources|
