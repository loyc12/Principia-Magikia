# Global Story Arc

**Type: Narrative planning**

**Status: Current direction**

## Rough Story Structure

|Arc|Primary Focus|
|---|---|
|I: Lost|Survival and loneliness|
|II: Stranger|Learning language and culture|
|III: Apprentice Scientist|First magical experiments|
|IV: Builder|Construction of first [[10 Magic System/02 Additional Disciplines/Golemancy\|golem]]|
|V: Recognition|Growing reputation|
|VI: Archive|Confronting established magical scholarship|
|VII: Revolution|Scientific and societal transformation|
|VIII: Legacy|Institutions, students, succession|

## Types of Conflict

|Conflict|Example|
|---|---|
|Survival|Wilderness, monsters|
|Social|Language barrier|
|Intellectual|Competing magical theories|
|Political|Knowledge monopolies|
|Economic|Funding research|
|Ethical|Publishing dangerous discoveries|
|Philosophical|Nature of consciousness|
|Personal|Balancing ambition and relationships|

## Important Story Beats

|Beat|Purpose|
|---|---|
|First night alone|Establish vulnerability|
|Acceptance that home is gone|Emotional turning point|
|First successful spell experiment|Birth of scientific mindset|
|First genuine conversation|Beginning of belonging|
|Camp destruction|Forces dependence on others|
|First paid invention|Research becomes sustainable|
|First functioning [[10 Magic System/02 Additional Disciplines/Golemancy\|golem]]|Beginning of longest relationship|
|First publication|Public recognition|
|First ideological opponent|Intellectual conflict|
|Golem disagrees|Recognition of true personhood|
|Archive disproven|Paradigm shift|
|Students surpass protagonist|Completion of legacy|

## Long-Term Narrative Escalation

|Scale|Main Question|
|---|---|
|Individual|Can I survive?|
|Social|Can I belong?|
|Intellectual|Can I understand?|
|Professional|Can I build?|
|Institutional|Can I convince others?|
|Civilizational|How should society change?|
|Historical|What legacy should remain?|
