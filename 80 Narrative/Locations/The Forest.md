# The Forest

**Type: Narrative planning**

**Status: Current direction**

Notes: 

Make [[The Forest|the forest]] feel almost prehistorically old within [[01 World/Cosmology/The Expanse]].

Not haunted, not evil, simply untamed.

A place where:

- no species are truly native
- magical interactions have accumulated for tens of thousands of years
- ecological equilibrium is extremely delicate

The locals enter reluctantly and by necessity.
