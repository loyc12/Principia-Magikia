# Different species

Think biologically. Different species should occupy different adaptive niches.

Maybe :

- One species evolved extraordinary magical sensitivity.
- Another has excellent endurance.
- Another matures in five years.
- Another lives 150 years.

Those biological differences naturally produce cultural differences, not stereotypes.
