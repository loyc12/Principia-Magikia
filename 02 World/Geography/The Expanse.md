The Expanse represent the entirety of the accessible world, and is the destination of the spatial anomalies which bring matter from the various sourceworlds.

As opposed to all known sourceworlds, the expanse is not composed of planetary body or a set of planes, but rather of a singular inverted sphere, the surface of which we inhabit, and containing at it centre a luminous singularity, which is analogous to the sun(s) or skylight(s) of sourceworlds. The expanse itself is thought to be perfectly spherical, and to have a radius of approximately {  2240 km }, making its surface area roughly { 63 million km$^2$ ( about 8 times smaller than earth's )}.

## Seasons

The singularity is not idle, but oscillate north and south by about { 315 km }, which is thought to be responsible for the seasonal variations between the two hemispheres, which cycle every { 19587 hours ( roughly 816 earth days )}. The distant poles are covered in dry deserts, with large temperature shifts based on the season, while the equator, meanwhile, remains at a mostly steady temperature year round.

However, the equator's climate experiences the strongest seasonal storms, which occur due to the movement of air and water between both hemispheres, which is strongest at the peak of the two season, when the cool, dry air and cooler waters encountering the of the cold hemisphere encounters the warm and humid air of the warm hemisphere, resulting in heavy winds and rainfall.

As a result of both these phenomena, the equator and poles are considered inadequate for permanent settlement, and thus, the civilise world exists in two narrower band between these two extremes.

## Nighttime

The singularity at its centre cycles between a day and night state every { 25.6 hours }, spending { 8.9 hours } dark, and taking { 1.3 hours } to change state at a steady, linear rate, leaving { 14.1 hours } of full illumination.

At night, it is possible to see luminous specks of various size rising from near the expanse's surface towards the singularity, getting brighter the higher they are, but remaining similarly dim to most sourceworld's nightskies. The singularity itself is surrounded by a dim, turbulent cloud of lights, often colloquially compared to a swarm of fireflies or a starry nebula.
