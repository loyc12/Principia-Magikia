# The Expanse

The **Expanse** represents the entirety of the accessible world and is the destination of the spatial anomalies that bring matter from various sourceworlds.

Unlike all known sourceworlds, the Expanse is neither a planetary body nor a simple plane. Instead, it consists of a single inverted sphere whose inner [[The Surface|surface]] is inhabited and whose centre contains a luminous [[The Singularity|Singularity]].

The Expanse contains both curved space-time and a distinct curved mana field. Their ordinary radial effects oppose one another: gravity draws matter and strongly potential-bearing mana outward towards the Surface, while free mana-field disruptions and weakly coupled Spent mana tend inward towards the Singularity. No exact inverse relationship between the two curvatures has been established.

The Expanse is thought to be perfectly spherical, with a radius of approximately { 2240 km } and a surface area of roughly { 63 million km$^2$ ( about 8 times smaller than Earth )}.

The Singularity's movement, illumination cycle, and the resulting seasons are described in [[The Singularity|its dedicated page]].

The Surface's properties and its various stratum are described in [[The Surface||its dedicated page]].
