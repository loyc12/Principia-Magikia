# The Expanse

The Expanse represents the entirety of the accessible world and is the destination of the spatial anomalies that bring matter from the various sourceworlds.

Unlike all known sourceworlds, the Expanse is neither a planetary body nor a set of planes. It is a single inverted sphere whose inner surface is inhabited and whose centre contains the luminous [[The Singularity|Singularity]]. The Expanse is thought to be perfectly spherical, with a radius of approximately { 2240 km } and a surface area of roughly { 63 million km$^2$ ( about 8 times smaller than Earth )}.

The Singularity's movement, illumination cycle, and the resulting seasons are described in [[The Singularity|its dedicated account]].
