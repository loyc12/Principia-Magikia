Mana is the magical energy which permeate all things in the expanse, and serves to fuel magic interactions. Mana itself naturally exists in two basic **undifferentiated states**, known as **primed mana** ( or *raw mana* ) and **spent mana** ( or *dead mana* ).

Primed mana may be **declined** into one or more primitive [[02 Mana Declensions|declensions]] through the action of a [[06 Mana Casters|Caster]] resulting in the conversion of said primed mana into both the desired declension(s), as well as an amount of spent mana, generated as a waste product of the declension process. This amount varies both with the declension(s) at play, its volume, its medium, as well as the technique used to accomplish it, but complete nullification of waste is thought to be impossible.

Absent external constraints, **differentiated primed declensions** ( often simply called **declined mana** or *impure declensions* ) gradually relax back into the undifferentiated state ( sometimes referred to as **undeclined mana**, or *pure mana* ). The rate of this relaxation depends on the declension, surrounding mana density, and the stability of the casting construct at hand, if any is present.

Of the various mana declensions ( including primed and spent mana, the two undifferentiated mana ), Primed and Spent mana are the most stable, as absent external force, they will remain in their undifferentiated state indefinitely.

Declined mana has distinct affinities to various materials based on its declension, such as biological matter and certain specific solid ( mainly crystals ) and fluids ( mainly water ). Certain material also seem to repel specific mana declensions ( thus impeding their natural flow ), and are thus primarily used for magical defence and precise mana flow control.

Primed mana and its declensions will instead tend to fall downward, away from the centre of [[The Expanse]], before pooling into low lying locations or specific mediums they may be attracted to. Inversely, Spent mana has a propensity to rise upwards, towards the singularity at where it is thought to be converted back into Primed mana through unknown means, before coming back down. 

Regardless of declension or state, mana will naturally diffuses from high to low concentration areas ( as would a gas in a given volume ), but also be repelled by other declensions to different degrees, with Primed mana being the easiest to repel, and Spent mana the hardest.

As pure mana is the most reactive of all declensions, it is attracted or repelled at various degrees to all materials which can affect any of the impure declensions, but rarely as much as at least one of said declensions, which often leads to Prime mana being "pushed out" of any medium with a strong affinity to a particular declension, and remain airborne.

Meanwhile, Spent mana is the least reactive mana declension, being near impossible to impede, observe, divert, repel, or otherwise interact with, and generally seeps out of wherever it was converted at noticed, which made its discovery particularly complex
