Wild casting is most often seen in creatures, but some plants are also documented as being able to cast mana. There is also rumours of certain sapiens or sapient races being able to do wild casting, but they remain unconfirmed.

The leading theory behind wild casting is the existence of dedicated organs, which fulfil the purpose of mana wells and catalysts, along an instinctive mental ability to cast magic itself. As such, wild casting would only be possible for being endowed with these body parts, and the instinctual nature of it would explain why lowly creatures are able to cast simple spells despite lacking The Spark.
