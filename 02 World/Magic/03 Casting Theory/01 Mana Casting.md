
**Mana casting** ( sometimes referred to as **spell casting**, mana shaping, or mana forming ) the controlled manipulation of one or more declensions of mana, via a [[02 Mana Constructs|mana construct]], to enact a change on a targeted reagent, which itself resides in the physical world.

This action inevitably requires four key components :

| Component     | Better definition                                                       |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reservoir** | The source or buffer of usable mana, be it declined or not              |
| **Catalyst**  | The interface that permits mana to be declined and coupled to the world |
| **Regulator** | The system that selects, regulates, and sequences the spell logic       |
| **Reagent**   | The physical or magical target whose state is altered                   |

These functionalities can often overlap, with the most common overlap being the combination of reservoirs, catalysts and process, which is how most modern casting instruments are designed, allowing their use by non-magi.

There exist four distinct methods of casting mana into a spell :

| Casting Method           |   Where the spell logic lives |                    Who/what executes the spell logic | Typical user                            |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------: | ---------------------------------------------------: | --------------------------------------- |
| **Wild Casting**         |            Biology / instinct |                     Body and instinctive mana organs | Beasts, rare feral individuals          |
| **Reflexive Casting**    | Internalized mental construct |                  A memorized “compiled” spell reflex | Ordinary people, novices                |
| **Structured Casting**   |  Actively assembled construct | Caster consciously builds/modifies the spell process | Educated mages, enchanters, researchers |
| **Instrumental Casting** |   External physical construct |                Tool executes most of the spell logic | Non-magi, artisans, soldiers            |
