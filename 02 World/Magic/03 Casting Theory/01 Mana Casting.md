
**Mana casting** ( sometimes referred to as *spell casting, mana shaping, mana forming, or simply magic* ) is the act of manipulating mana itself to enact change upon the physical world. This can be done in various ways, but inevitably requires four components :

- a reservoir ( which serves to hold the mana )
- a catalyst ( which serves to channel the mana )
- a caster ( which guides the declension and application of mana )
- a reagent ( which is affected by the mana )

These functionalities can often overlap, with the most common overlap being the combination of reservoirs, catalysts and casting devices, which is how most modern casting constructs are designed, allowing their use by non-magi.

There exist three distinct methods of casting mana :

- Wild Casting ( most often seen in magical beasts )
- Reflexive Casting ( most widespread form of magic )
- Structured Casting ( most powerful form of magic )
