**Reflexive Casting** ( sometimes called *cantrip casting* ) is the act of funnelling mana into basic mental casting constructs which have been learned beforehand, before letting said construct decline and cast the desired effect ( known as a **spell** ) onto a reagent.

This is the method most magi novices default to using. While is it comparatively simple, as the user only has minimal casting to do themselves ( just enough to funnel the mana into the construct ), it is strictly dependent on a preexisting mental construct, which are themselves overwhelmingly designed via structured casting.

It is the most widely practised form of magic, and most people at least know a few cantrips by the time they turn 10 season old, although this fact is most prevalent in cities and larger towns.

The most perform cantrips in the expense are, by far, the Illumination Spell and the Heating Spell, followed by the Invisible Hand Spell, although their usage as begun to decline in recent times due to the commercialisation of simple cantrip casting constructs, which alleviate the mental load of casting itself.

It is most often seen in magical warfare and purpose built tooling, and the casting construct itself is generally very sensitive to physical or magical disruption, especially when not properly encased in mana repealing materials.
