
## Principles of Mana

### Principle I — Conservation

 Mana is never created nor destroyed. All magical phenomena arise from the redistribution, transformation, or declension of preexisting mana.

### Principle II — Primitive Operations

Mana possesses no intrinsic affinity toward any observable phenomenon. Instead, it may only be declined into one of several primitive operational modes, each authorising a single class of transformations upon reality.

### Principle III — Composability

Any magical phenomenon may be expressed as the sequential or concurrent composition of one or more primitive operations.

### Principle IV — Natural Propagation

Mana directly performs only its primitive operation. All subsequent consequences arise through the ordinary laws governing the affected reagent.
