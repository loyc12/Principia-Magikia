## Law of Permutation

Mana naturally exists in an undifferentiated state known as **raw mana** ( also known as prime, pure, or undeclined mana ).

Raw mana may be **declined** into one or more primitive [[02 Mana Declensions|declensions]] through the action of a caster.

Absent external constraints, declined mana gradually relaxes back toward its undifferentiated state.

The rate of this relaxation depends on the declension, surrounding mana density, and the stability of the casting construct.
