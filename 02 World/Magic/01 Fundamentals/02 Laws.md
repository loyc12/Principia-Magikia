## Law of Permutation

Mana naturally exists in two basic states, known as **primed mana** ( also known as *raw mana* ) and **spent mana** ( also known as *dead mana* ). Up until recently, spent mana had been hypothetical in nature, but recent experiments confirmed its existence.

Primed mana may be **declined** into one or more primitive [[02 Mana Declensions|declensions]] through the action of a caster, resulting in the conversion of said primed mana into both the desired declension(s), as well as a ratio of spent mana, generated as a waste product of the declension process.

Absent external constraints, declined mana pools downwards and gradually relaxes back toward its undifferentiated, primed state. The rate of this relaxation depends on the declension, surrounding mana density, and the stability of the casting construct.

Meanwhile, it is believed that spent mana, which is much harder to observe due to its near-inert state, rises upwards, towards the centre of [[The Expanse]], where it is converted back into Primed mana through unknown means.
