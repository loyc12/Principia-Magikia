
**Mana casting** ( sometimes referred to as **spell casting**, mana shaping, or mana forming ) the controlled manipulation of one or more declensions of mana, via a [[04 Mana Constructs|mana construct]], to enact a change on a targeted reagent, which itself resides in the physical world.

People able to cast magic are most often referred to as **magi** ( singular : **magus** ), mages, magicians, wizard, witches, or simply **casters**

## Casting Components

All spell casting requires five key components :

| Casting Component | Definition                                                                                   |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reservoir**     | The source or buffer of usable mana, be it declined or not                                   |
| **Conduit**       | The designated travel and triage pathways for mana                                           |
| **Catalyst**      | The interface that permits mana to be declined into operational form                         |
| **Operator**      | The mind, organ, construct, or device that selects, sequences, and regulates the spell logic |
| **Reagent**       | The physical, material target whose state is altered                                         |

These casting components are the physical parts at play during spell casting, and are not to be confused with Construct components, which generally live in the operator itself.

## Casting Method

There exist four distinct methods of casting mana into a spell :

| Casting Method           | Where the spell logic lives   | Who/what executes the spell logic                                        | Typical user                                       |
| ------------------------ | :---------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Wild Casting**         | Biology / instinct            | Body and instinctive mana organs                                         | Beasts, rare feral individuals                     |
| **Reflexive Casting**    | Internalized mental construct | Caster memorises an immutable, “compiled” spell into a reflexive pattern | Ordinary people, novice magi                       |
| **Structured Casting**   | Actively assembled construct  | Caster consciously builds and adjusts a custom spell  pattern            | Advanced magi, invocaters, enchanters, researchers |
| **Instrumental Casting** | External physical construct   | Tool executes all of the spell logic                                     | Non-magi, artisans, soldiers                       |

## Casting Steps

Regardless of the casting method, all spell go through these 10 steps :

| Step             | Description                                                          | Failure Type                                                             |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Selection**    | Operator determines required declension                              | wrong declension selected                                                |
| **Delimitation** | Reagent and boundaries are identified                                | wrong target delimited                                                   |
| **Sourcing**     | Usable mana is drawn from a reservoir                                | insufficient mana, caster fatigue                                        |
| **Funnelling**   | Mana is moved through a conduit or caster                            | leakage, burns, construct collapse                                       |
| **Declension**   | Primed mana is converted into operational form                       | undesired mana declension or leakage waste                               |
| **Application**  | Declined mana performs primitive operations on the reagent           | unexpected disruptions, backlash, or reagent state impede the operations |
| **Regulation**   | The construct constrains magnitude, duration, and feedback           | overpowered or underpowered spell                                        |
| **Propagation**  | Spell's mundane physical consequences unfold, spent mana is permuted | undesired side effects of spell                                          |
| **Termination**  | Mana flow is stopped by the operator                                 | runaway effect                                                           |
| **Relaxation**   | Spent mana and unused mana disperse                                  | excess mana wasted                                                       |
