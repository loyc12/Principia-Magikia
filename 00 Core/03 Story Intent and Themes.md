# Story Intent and Themes

**Type: Core reference**

**Status: Current foundation**

This note combines the former Thesis and Themes notes. It preserves their central claim, supporting ideas, thematic axes, and open questions.

## Core Thesis

Civilizations advance not merely through intelligence or powerful discoveries, but through the patient construction of reliable knowledge, open collaboration, and relationships built on mutual understanding.

## Secondary Ideas

### Magic is physics

Magic is empirically knowable and materially manipulable, making it a powerful tool when properly researched and understood.

### People are not stupid

Characters and organizations have discoverable goals, preferences, and incentives, and act towards fulfilling them to the best of their abilities and knowledge.

### Organizations are not monoliths

Organizations and polities are made of people and their relations, and thus host internal tensions and divergent incentives, which inform their observable behaviour.

### Ideas are born out of conditions

Cultures and ideologies are shaped by material conditions, generating selection pressure based on their suitability to the societies that contain them.

| Axis         | Position A                | Position B                     |
| ------------ | ------------------------- | ------------------------------ |

| Science      | Empirical experimentation | Traditional authority          |
| Magic        | Understand and engineer   | Respect and preserve           |
| Society      | Individual innovation     | Collective stability           |
| Progress     | Continuous advancement    | Careful restraint              |
| Identity     | Origin defines you        | Choices define you             |
| Intelligence | Consciousness emerges     | Consciousness is innate/sacred |
| Nature       | Humanity reshapes nature  | Humanity adapts to nature      |

## Main Questions

### How should knowledge be gathered?

Empirical experimentation vs Prevalence of dogma

### How should knowledge be shared?

Open dissemination vs Restricted expertise

### How should nature be treated?

Manage and exploit vs Protect and maintain

### How should magic be treated?

Study and manipulate vs Worship and safeguard

### Where should progress emerge from?

Individual innovations vs Collective decisions

### How should society be organized?

Egalitarian vs Hierarchical

### What defines personhood?

Observable behaviour vs Intrinsic origins

### Are traditions valuable?

Discount preconceptions vs Venerate the past
